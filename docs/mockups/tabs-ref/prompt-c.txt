Game UI concept sheet for RuneSkin, a Valheim UI skin. Style references in this folder: ref-inv-a.png, ref-stats-a.png, ref-hud-2-final.png (approved look: thin dark carved-stone frames with Norse knotwork corners, near-black translucent glass fill, small cyan rune glow accents, gold-trimmed header plaque, Cormorant small-caps labels in pale bone white). Current tab sprites (to be replaced, they read flat): tab_normal.png, tab_selected.png, tab_hover.png.
Draw ONE 1920x1080 sheet on a dark blurred Nordic dusk background, showing the TAB SET of this kit attached to the top edge of a dark glass panel matching ref-inv-a.png:
- Row 1: a horizontal strip of 4 labelled tabs "CRAFT", "UPGRADE", "STASH", "BOUNTY" sitting on the panel top edge; tab 1 SELECTED, tab 2 HOVER, tabs 3-4 NORMAL. The selected tab merges into the panel (no frame line under it); normal tabs sit slightly lower and darker.
- Row 2 (right side): a vertical tab column of 4 tabs on a panel's left edge, same three states.
- Row 3: the three states of a single tab enlarged 2x side by side with captions "normal", "hover", "selected", plus one small square icon-only tab in the three states.
Tabs must have a forged bevel (raised stone edge catching light from the top-left, inner shadow), the same frame thickness and palette as the reference panel frames, readable labels, no text other than the labels and captions. Crisp UI art, orthographic, no perspective, no characters.
Variant C "glass and rune": slim tab of dark translucent glass inside a thin stone rim; selected = full-width cyan rune underline that flows into the panel and a glowing small rune glyph left of the label; hover = dim cyan underline; normal = no underline.
